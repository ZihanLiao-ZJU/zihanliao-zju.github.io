My research focuses on developing general-purpose Monte Carlo simulation (MCS) algorithms for reliability estimation, Bayesian updating, and optimization. I am also interested in applying machine learning to engineering data and chemical spectra.

The research and data collection underlying my publications were carried out under the guidance of Professor Binbin Li at Zhejiang University. I gratefully acknowledge his support and guidance.
